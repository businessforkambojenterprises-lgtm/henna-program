        4 · Surface check

        Before running anything, measure the real surface at three points with the paper feeler. If the camera error is the same everywhere, the path is raised by the smallest measured amount.

        Paper readings

        At each point: send the commands one by one, slide a paper strip under the tip (hold the far end), step down 0.1 mm at a time until the paper drags, then send ? and paste the line.

          6 · Run & verify

          Run the program in UGS, then measure the real gap at three points with the paper feeler.

          Run

          1. Make sure the hand has not moved. Home only if there was an alarm or reset ($HZ then $H).
          2. Open the program in UGS. Film from the side at palm height.
          3. Stand at the emergency stop and press Start. Stop at once if the tip touches.

          Gap check

          After the run: at each point, lower to the program height, then step down with the paper until it drags, and paste the ? line.
