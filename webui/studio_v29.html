    Set up this machine

    Two measurements before calibrating. 1: the work area (from the controller settings, checked with a ruler) - this unlocks X/Y jog. 2: jog the camera over the hand area (live picture in a job's Scan step), measure the nozzle tip above the bed at Z 0, then save.

    Calibrate this machine

    A: X/Y from the dot sheet. B: Z from paper-strip touches on a flat block. Each fit is a draft; approving both unlocks the machine.

    X/Y nudge (any height)

    A · X/Y

    Tape the dot sheet flat, Move to scan position (job Scan step or Daily check card), then record. Then for each dot: jog at travel Z until the tip is above it, lower with Z− until just above the paper, check from the front and the side, save.

    B · Z

    Remove the sheet, put the flat block under the camera, Move to scan position, record. Then at 3 or 4 spots on the block: jog above it at travel Z, lower with Z− (0.1 at the end) until the paper strip is just pinched, save.

        4 · Program

        The hover program is written at the nozzle height and speed you chose. Between strokes the nozzle only hops 5 mm above the highest skin under the crossing. A separate checker re-reads it. Studio runs it in the next step.

          Confirm

          Programs are for the prosthetic hand only.

          Default 600 mm/min; 300 to 1200 allowed. The first run at a new speed should be an air run (no hand). The drop down to the hand always stays at 300.
          Paste ON after the drop to the skin, OFF before every lift; the start delay lets the paste reach the nozzle before it moves. Both dispensers are on the controller's spindle output (laser mode must be off, $32=0).

          New job

          What is under the camera?

          Home the machine

          Sends $HZ (Z up first), then $H. The head moves to its home corner.

          Move to scan position

          Remove all hands and objects from the travel area before moving. The head rises to travel height, moves across, then lowers to the scan position.

          Start the hover run

          The head rises to travel height, then follows the design at the nozzle height you chose above the measured skin. No dispensing. Before it starts, the camera checks that the hand is where it was scanned; on a real hand it keeps watching and pauses the machine if the hand moves.

          Jog

          Moves stay at travel height (Z -20) or higher, so the nozzle cannot reach a hand. Below Z -20 only Z up works. Only a checked program lowers the nozzle.
